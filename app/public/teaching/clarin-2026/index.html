Eleven ways into the material.

Each activity has a demonstration, complete texts and prompts, saved results and detailed explanations. App links take you to the existing tools when you want to try an analysis.

01 / RhymeFew-shot examples, stress, mosaic rhyme and phonological verification. 02 / Emotions & NeSyAchilles, a Cairns-inspired prompt and four forms of symbolic intervention. 03 / ZeugmaThucydides, two complete graphs and six recorded Prolog queries. 04 / NatureA landscape, a metaphor and Papadiamantis: deciding what counts. 05 / Voyant-NLPConcordances, spelling choices and a sentiment result to question. 06 / SvarnaThe separate corpus workbench: contexts, varieties and proposed LLM inputs. 07 / PlotAnalyzerFollow a theory-guided reading, symbolic rules and model feedback. 08 / TermGuardCorpus evidence and the difference between a well-formed and a useful definition. 09 / Linguistic DistanceChoose dimensions, inspect a changing mean and keep missing data visible. 10 / Dialect GeneratorFour recorded generations, exact prompts and questions about dialectal evidence. 11 / NATSA network edge can connect a denied encounter.
